# Changelog

- 2026-10-14, 1.1.0: Fair prices can now be worked out four ways (multiplicative, additive, power and Shin, in a new fairprice module), with Shin as the default and every fair price labeled with its method. A new EV gate setting, worst case by default, counts a price as an edge only when it beats fair under all four: the Edges list, highlights, alerts and every Bet or Pass follow it.
